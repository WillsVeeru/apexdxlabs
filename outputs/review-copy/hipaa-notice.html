Your Information. Your Rights. Our Responsibilities

This notice describes how medical information about you may be used and disclosed by Apex DX Labs and how you can access this information. Please review it carefully.

1. Our Responsibilities

Apex DX Labs is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your protected health information (PHI). PHI may include information that identifies you and relates to your health, healthcare services, laboratory testing, or payment for healthcare. Examples may include your name, date of birth, test orders, specimen information, laboratory results, ordering-provider information, and billing records.

Where HIPAA applies, we are required to maintain the privacy of your PHI; provide this notice describing our legal duties and privacy practices; follow the terms of the notice currently in effect; notify you when a breach of unsecured PHI requires notification under applicable law; and provide you with the rights described in this notice, subject to applicable legal exceptions.

2. How We May Use and Disclose Your Information

Treatment

We may use or disclose your PHI to perform laboratory testing and report results to the ordering healthcare provider or other authorized healthcare professional involved in your care.

Payment

We may use or disclose PHI to bill for laboratory services, collect payment, verify coverage, process claims, and perform other payment-related activities permitted by law.

Healthcare Operations

We may use or disclose PHI for quality assurance, laboratory operations, auditing, accreditation, compliance, training, and other healthcare operations permitted by law.

Service Providers and Business Associates

We may disclose PHI to vendors and service providers who assist us with permitted activities, such as laboratory information systems, billing, information technology, specimen transportation, or secure records management. Where required, these parties must agree to protect PHI under applicable law and written agreements.

Individuals Involved in Your Care

Where permitted by law, we may share relevant information with a person involved in your care or payment for your care, consistent with your wishes or other applicable legal requirements.

Legal Requirements and Public Health

We may disclose PHI when required or permitted by law, including certain disclosures to public health authorities, health oversight agencies, government authorities, courts, and law enforcement agencies.

Health and Safety

We may disclose PHI in circumstances permitted by law to address serious threats to health or safety or to support legally authorized public health activities.

Other Permitted Disclosures

Other disclosures may be permitted for activities such as medical examiners, coroners, workers’ compensation, certain research activities, and other purposes specifically authorized by applicable law.

We will not use or disclose PHI for a purpose requiring your authorization unless the required authorization has been obtained. You may revoke an authorization in writing as permitted by law, but revocation will not affect actions already taken in reliance on the authorization.

Certain records, including records protected under federal substance-use-disorder confidentiality laws, may be subject to additional restrictions.

3. Your Rights Regarding Your Health Information

Obtain a Copy

Subject to applicable law and its exceptions, you may request access to or a copy of PHI maintained by us. We may provide the information in paper or electronic form as required by law. Permitted fees may apply.

Request a Correction

You may ask us to amend information you believe is incorrect or incomplete. We may deny a request in certain circumstances permitted by law and will provide the required explanation.

Request Confidential Communications

You may ask us to communicate with you through a particular method or at a specific address. We will accommodate reasonable requests as required by applicable law.

Request Restrictions

You may ask us to restrict certain uses or disclosures of your PHI. We are not required to agree to every request, except where applicable law requires a restriction.

Request an Accounting of Certain Disclosures

You may ask for a list of certain disclosures of your PHI, subject to the exceptions and time limits established by law.

Obtain a Copy of This Notice

You may request a paper copy of this notice at any time, even if you received it electronically.

Choose a Personal Representative

Where legally authorized, you may designate or have a personal representative act on your behalf concerning your PHI.

File a Complaint

You may complain to us or to the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services if you believe your privacy rights have been violated. We will not retaliate against you for filing a complaint.
